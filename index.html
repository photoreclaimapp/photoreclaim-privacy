Privacy Policy for PhotoReclaim

Last updated: August 6, 2026

PhotoReclaim ("the app") is built around a simple principle: your photos are yours, and they never leave your device.

What we collect

Nothing. PhotoReclaim does not collect, transmit, store, or have access to any of your personal data, photos, or usage information. The app has no backend server, makes no network requests related to your photo library, and includes no analytics or advertising software of any kind.

How the app works

All photo analysis — finding screenshots, comparing photos for similarity, measuring blur, and reading text in receipts — happens entirely on your iPhone, using Apple's on-device PhotoKit and Vision frameworks. Your photos and videos are never uploaded anywhere, by us or anyone else.

Photo Library access

PhotoReclaim requests access to your Photos library solely to display your photos within the app and to let you choose which ones to delete. This access is used only while the app is open and only for the features described above. You can review or revoke this permission at any time in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → PhotoReclaim.

Deletions

When you choose to delete photos or videos, PhotoReclaim uses Apple's standard system deletion, which moves items to your Photos app's "Recently Deleted" album, where they remain recoverable for 30 days — the same as deleting directly within the Photos app. PhotoReclaim cannot recover items after that 30-day window, as it has no separate copy of anything you delete.

In-app purchase

PhotoReclaim offers a single one-time purchase to unlock the deletion feature, processed entirely through Apple's App Store / StoreKit. We do not receive or store your payment information — that's handled directly by Apple, per Apple's own privacy practices.

Notifications

If you enable notifications, PhotoReclaim may send a local notification when a photo scan finishes. This notification is generated entirely on your device and involves no external server.

Third parties

PhotoReclaim does not share data with third parties, because it does not collect any data to share.

Children's privacy

PhotoReclaim does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, an updated version will be posted at this same URL with a revised "Last updated" date.
