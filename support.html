PhotoReclaim Support

Help with using the app, answers to common questions, and how to reach us.

How PhotoReclaim works

  1. Choose how far back to scan — start with Last Month for a quick first look, or All Photos for everything.
  2. Wait while PhotoReclaim looks at your photos — everything happens on your iPhone, nothing is uploaded. A large library can take a few minutes; your screen stays on while it works, and you'll get a notification the moment it's done.
  3. Open a category on the dashboard, like Screenshots or Blurry Photos, to review what was found.
  4. Tap photos to select them for deletion. A red border means selected. Use Select All or Select Suggested to go faster.
  5. In Burst and Similar groups, the photo marked Keep is the best shot — it can never be deleted.
  6. Tap Review & Delete, check the summary, and confirm. iOS shows one final system confirmation.
  7. Changed your mind? Deleted items stay in Recently Deleted in the Photos app for 30 days.

This same walkthrough is always available inside the app: tap the gear icon on any screen → Instructions.

Frequently asked questions

Is anything ever uploaded or shared?
No. PhotoReclaim has no backend server. Every scan — screenshots, blur detection, similar-photo comparison, receipt text recognition — runs entirely on your iPhone using Apple's own on-device frameworks. Your photos never leave your device.
I deleted something by mistake. Can I get it back?
Yes, for 30 days. PhotoReclaim uses the same deletion mechanism as the Photos app itself, so anything removed goes to Photos → Albums → Recently Deleted and can be restored from there like any other deletion, no different from deleting a photo manually.
Why does the first scan take a while?
Looking closely at each photo — measuring sharpness, comparing near-duplicates, reading text in receipts — takes real processing time, especially on a large library. Starting with a smaller range (Last Month or Last 15 Days) gives you results in seconds; scanning your whole library is more thorough but takes longer. Your screen stays on during a scan so it isn't interrupted by auto-lock, and you'll get a notification when it finishes.
Does PhotoReclaim choose what to delete for me?
No. Nothing is ever staged for deletion on its own. You select every photo yourself — "Select Suggested" pre-highlights the app's more confident picks within a group as a shortcut, but even then nothing is deleted until you review the full list and confirm.
How do I change the app's language?
PhotoReclaim automatically matches your iPhone's language and currently supports 14 languages. To use a different one, open the gear icon → Language, tap the language you want, then choose it on the app's page in iOS Settings — this is how per-app language switching works on iOS.
I paid once — why is it asking me to unlock again on another device?
Purchases are tied to your Apple ID, not a specific iPhone. Tap Restore Purchase on the unlock screen while signed into the same Apple ID you originally purchased with, and access will be restored at no additional cost.
Is this a subscription?
No. PhotoReclaim is a single one-time purchase that unlocks deletion permanently. There is no recurring charge, ever. Scanning and browsing every category is free before you buy anything.
